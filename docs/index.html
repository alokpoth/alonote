FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my notes stored? +
All your notes, photos, and voice recordings are stored exclusively inside your device's private sandboxed SQLite database and local files. No remote server ever receives your data.
How do I backup or transfer my notes? +
From Settings > Backup & Restore, tap "Export Backup" to generate a complete JSON file, or export individual notes to PDF or Markdown (.md). You can save it to a USB drive, computer, or personal storage.
Is AloNote truly free and ad-free? +
Yes. AloNote is an independent initiative by Alokpath. It is and will always remain free, open-source under the MIT license, and completely tracker-free.
