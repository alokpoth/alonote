🌓 Light & Dark Theme

Seamless toggle between monochrome pure black Dark Mode and paper-white Light Mode, respecting your system preferences automatically.

📖 Distraction-Free Reader Mode

Switch with one tap to a beautiful, clean typography reading view with interactive checkboxes that can be checked off in real time.

↺ Dedicated Undo & Redo

Effortlessly undo or redo accidental typing mistakes and formatting changes right from the mobile toolbar.

📊 Detailed Note Insights

Live word, character, paragraph, checklist completion, and estimated reading and speaking time metrics with one tap.

↕️ Drag-and-Drop Reorder

Press and hold any note card to drag up or down to reorder your thoughts with buttery smooth 120 FPS animations and instant persistence.

🗑️ Recycle Bin & Soft Delete

Accidentally deleted a note? Safely restore your notes anytime from the Recycle Bin with instant undo toasts.

📝 Live Markdown & Checklists

Docked Apple-style formatting toolbar for Bold, Italic, Headings, Bullet Lists, and To-Do Checklists with smart auto-continuation on Enter.

🔒 100% Offline & Private

No internet permission, zero tracking SDKs, no accounts, and no cloud synchronization. Your private notes stay exclusively in your device's local Room database.
