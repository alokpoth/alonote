FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my notes stored? +
All your notes, photos, and voice recordings are stored exclusively inside your device's private sandboxed SQLite database and local files. No remote server ever receives your data.
How do I backup or transfer my notes? +
From Settings > Backup & Restore, tap "Export Backup" to generate a complete JSON file, or export individual notes to PDF or Markdown (.md). You can save it to a USB drive, computer, or personal storage.
Is AloNote truly free and ad-free? +
Yes. AloNote is an independent initiative by Alokpath. It is and will always remain free, tracker-free, open-source, and licensed for personal use under the Alokpath Sovereign License.
Is downloading and installing AloNote APK safe? +
AloNote is completely safe, transparent, and private. The app requests zero internet permissions (android.permission.INTERNET is entirely absent), meaning it is technically incapable of sending data over any network. Your notes and files stay strictly on your local device.
